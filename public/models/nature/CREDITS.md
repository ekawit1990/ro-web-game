# Nature models

Ultimate Stylized Nature Pack (May 2022) by **Quaternius** — https://quaternius.com

License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Converted from the pack's OBJ files to geometry-only GLB (gzipped); textures downsized to 128–512 px and
matched to meshes by material name at runtime (src/three/nature.ts).
Support the author: https://www.patreon.com/quaternius

## M_*.glb.gz (2026-11-22)
